# Incident checklist

- [ ] Severity assigned and incident owner named
- [ ] Primary/secondary on-call and escalation route confirmed
- [ ] Impact, affected users, and data classification recorded
- [ ] Containment or mitigation started; evidence preserved without PII
- [ ] Customer/internal communication sent at the required cadence
- [ ] Recovery verified with health checks and critical journey smoke tests
- [ ] Rollback or forward-fix decision recorded
- [ ] Post-incident review scheduled for major incidents under the severity matrix
- [ ] Action items have owners and due dates
- [ ] Regulatory or privacy notification assessed
